# THE SCENE AI — Game Plan
### What you're doing right · What to get rid of · What to add
*Drafted July 2026, based on your product, your brand kit, and the competitor study.*

---

## ✅ WHAT YOU'RE DOING RIGHT (keep and lean in)

1. **Character-first + continuity as a system.** Nobody else in the study
   teaches identity lock + world continuity. Prompt-pack sellers make one
   pretty girl; you make a character people follow like a TV show. This is
   the product's spine — lead every pitch with it.

2. **Culturally accurate packs.** The Black/BIPOC-centered hair, skin and
   outfit systems (with texture and identity protection) serve the biggest,
   most underserved slice of the "AI baddie" market. That's a moat, not a
   feature. Say it on the sales page in exactly those words.

3. **Copy-paste-first design.** Every prompt is a labeled COPY THIS panel.
   Buyers don't want to learn prompting — they want to paste and post.
   You built for how people actually behave.

4. **The road map / "part 4 of 19" structure.** A finishable product is
   rare in this niche. Competitors sell scattered lessons; yours tells
   people exactly where they are and where to go next. Finishers become
   testimonials.

5. **The brand.** Black + silver + cyan/magenta with the bracket motif
   looks premium. Yeltsov aside, everyone else in this market looks like a
   Canva template. Your product LOOKS like the price you want to charge.

6. **The keyword-funnel instinct.** "Comment SCENE" is the right mechanic
   — the top funnel creators in the study run the exact same play. Keep
   SCENE as *your* keyword on *your* content. (It's out of the buyers'
   templates now — they build their own.)

7. **A product suite, not a one-off.** SCENE AI + The Female Outfit Pack
   shows you think in catalogs. That's the foundation for the membership
   model below.

8. **Honesty in the teaching.** The product says "viral is not a promise —
   design the conditions." Keep that. It protects refunds and reputation
   in a niche full of income hype.

9. **Simple enough to finish.** Sixth-grade reading level, big type, one
   color per section. "The easiest AI system you've ever used" is now a
   TRUE claim — make it your tagline in ads.

---

## ❌ WHAT TO GET RID OF

1. **Your funnel inside the paid product.** DONE — "Comment SCENE,"
   "DM me RICH," and your booking links are out of the buyer templates.
   Rule going forward: *your* keywords live in *your* content, never in
   the product.

2. **Your niche inside the general product.** DONE — lash/sanitation sets
   and Destyni-specific case files are out. Your beauty content is proof
   for YOUR account; the product stays niche-neutral so every buyer sees
   themselves in it.

3. **City-specific and personal callouts in templates.** DONE — "Chicago /
   Atlanta brands" became "[YOUR CITY] brands." Keep auditing new content
   for anything only YOU can say.

4. **The one-giant-PDF format.** DONE — 19 branded modules beat one
   83-page bundle. Never ship the monolith again.

5. **One-price, one-product thinking.** A single $X product caps you.
   The study shows the market supports a ladder up to $4,997 (Lyttle).
   Retire the idea that SCENE is "a PDF you sell once."

6. **Teaching without showing.** Drop any promo that explains the system
   without the character on screen. Proof-first or don't post it.

---

## ➕ WHAT TO ADD (in order)

### Now — before/at launch
1. **Run the system on your own account.** Create YOUR character, post
   the episodes, screenshot everything. The product's best ad is its own
   output. Every competitor with traction leads with proof, not promises.
2. **A free starter drop (lead magnet).** "Make your first AI character
   in 1 hour" — Prompt 1F, one hook, one caption, one pinned comment.
   Delivered when someone comments SCENE. It samples the product and
   builds your list at the same time.
3. **A tool-stack quickstart page.** The PDFs say "your AI tool" — buyers
   will ask "which app?" Add one page: the 2–3 tools you actually use for
   faces, video, and editing, with free/paid options. (I can build this
   page into Start Here when you name your stack.)
4. **Launch content calendar.** Two weeks of proof-first hooks from the
   study: her numbers on screen → one-line lesson → "Comment SCENE."
   Post into the live trends: "AI baddie," "AI influencer," "making a
   baddie AI."

### Next 30–60 days
5. **Community layer.** Private Telegram/Discord or IG broadcast channel
   for buyers (Yeltsov's retention engine). Weekly prompt drop keeps it
   alive with almost no work.
6. **Video walkthroughs.** Screen-record yourself running each module
   once. PDFs + videos justify the next price tier and cut refunds.
7. **A wins loop.** The Episode File worksheets already capture results —
   add "share your episode with #THESCENEAI" so testimonials collect
   themselves. Repost the best ones; that's your social proof flywheel.

### The ladder (60–90 days, from the Lyttle playbook)
8. **Free live challenge** ("Build her in a weekend") → fills the list.
9. **SCENE AI core** → the 19-module system (current product).
10. **THE SCENE AI membership** → monthly drops: new scene libraries,
    seasonal outfit/hair packs, new hook vaults, community. Recurring
    revenue; your catalog instinct already fits this.
11. **Premium tier** → 1:1 creative direction or a small-group
    "Director's Room." The market's ceiling is ~$5K; you don't need it
    at launch, but the ladder should point there.

---

### The one-line strategy
**Show the character, run your keyword, sell the system, then build the
ladder.** Everything above is just those four moves in order.
