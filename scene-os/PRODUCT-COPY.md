## Notes on the draft (what changed and why)

1. **Added anchor pricing.** Every product on both live stores shows
   $X ~~$Y~~. Non-optional.
2. **Split card copy from page copy.** The 19-item list was a wall on a
   store card; cards on both live stores are 1–2 lines.
3. **Grouped the 19 modules into 4 phases.** Scannable on mobile; the
   count ("19-part") still carries the value.
4. **Merged the two overlapping lists.** The draft had "you'll get" (19
   items) AND "learn how to" (8 items); now outcomes come first, contents
   second — the order both stores use (transformation, then deliverables).
5. **Added the ease claim.** "Copy and paste / sixth grader" is your
   differentiator vs every competitor and wasn't in the draft.
6. **Kept "all sales are final"** as you wrote it — standard for instant
   digital products on Stan. One flag: top course sellers at $97+ often
   show a guarantee instead (risk reversal converts better). Options:
   keep sales-final (safest for a downloadable), or offer a 7-day
   "read Start Here — if you don't build your character, money back."
   Your call; the free starter drop softens the risk either way.
7. **Get stars fast.** Both stores show ★5.0 on almost every card. Give
   3–5 early buyers the product in exchange for an honest review the
   first week.
