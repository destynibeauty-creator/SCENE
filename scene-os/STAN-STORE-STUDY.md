# Stan Store Study — Highest-Converting Stores In & Around Your Niche
*Researched July 2026. Stan blocks automated page-scraping, so store layouts
and prices below come from public articles, search listings and Stan's own
blog. Before you build: open the four niche stores at the bottom in your
app and screenshot their product order + prices — 10 minutes, worth it.*

---

## 1 · The proven top converters on Stan (any niche)

### Abigail Peugh — first creator to $1M on Stan
- **Model:** ALL low-ticket, from day one. 14,000+ customers, $40–50K/mo —
  the same numbers her high-ticket friends make, with none of the sales calls.
- **Core product:** "Digital Product Starter Kit" — a 25-day guide.
- **Why she converts:** cheap enough to buy on impulse from one video,
  volume covers the rest. Her store IS her funnel: free thing on top,
  low-ticket core, everything email-captured.

### Jenna Peterson — $2.5M+ on Stan
- **Model:** the LADDER. Courses and coaching from **$67 up to $1,100**
  ("The Social Academy" $197–$1,100), 1:1 and small-group on top.
- **Why she converts:** every budget has a next step. Nobody bounces
  because there was nothing at their price.

### Rachel Adams Lee — Stan's own funnel case study
- **Her rules, verbatim from Stan's blog:**
  - Order products **cheapest → most expensive** down the page.
  - **End every price in 7 or 9** (never round numbers).
  - Always have an **order bump** at checkout — "they already decided to
    buy; that's the highest-intent moment in the journey."
  - Balance **upsells AND downsells** so there's always a next step no
    matter the budget.
  - Write copy "like you're talking to a friend over coffee."

### The platform-wide pattern (from Stan's $500M in creator sales)
1. **Email capture on the front end** — every top store gives something
   free first. Followers churn; the list is the asset.
2. **Funnel, not shelf** — lead magnet → core offer → upsell/downsell
   branches. Stores that are just a menu of products convert far worse.
3. **Transformation, not information** — top stores sell "what you'll
   become," not "what's inside."
4. **Proof density** — checkout pages carry testimonials/screenshots;
   benchmark example: a $9 guide with a $19 order bump sees ~30% take rate.

monetization layer. You're not entering their market — you're the upgrade
their buyers look for after the prompt pack disappoints.

## 3 · Your Stan store blueprint (build it in this order)

**Bio line (the promise, not the product):**
> "Turn ONE AI character into a series people follow — the easiest AI
> system you've ever used. Start free below. 👇"

**Product stack, top to bottom (cheapest → priciest, prices end in 7):**

1. **FREE — "Your First AI Character in 1 Hour"** *(email required)*
   Prompt 1F/1M + one hook + one caption + one pinned comment from the
   system. Delivered by DM automation when they **comment SCENE** on any
   post, and sitting on top of the store for everyone else.

2. **$17 — "The Identity Lock Mini-Pack"** *(tripwire)*
   20 identity-locked photo prompts (pull from Modules 02/09/10 material).
   This catches the exact impulse buyer who'd otherwise buy
   @annetheaicreator's pack — then the checkout order-bump and upsell walk
   them up to the system.
   - **Order bump at its checkout ($27):** The Female Outfit Pack.

3. **$97 — THE SCENE AI (launch price; $147 after)** *(core offer)*
   All 19 modules. Sales page = transformation copy: "her face never
   changes — that's why they follow," culturally accurate by design,
   6th-grade simple, copy-paste everything. Add a 7-day "read Start Here,
   build your character — or your money back" guarantee (top stores all
   carry risk reversal).
   - **Order bump at checkout ($17–27):** the Mini-Pack or Outfit Pack
     (whichever they don't own).
   - **Upsell after purchase ($37/mo):** **SCENE Society** — monthly scene
     libraries, seasonal outfit/hair drops, community. (This is the
     recurring-revenue layer Jenna/Abigail-tier stores live on.)
   - **Downsell if they skip the core ($27):** Outfit Pack or Mini-Pack —
     "not ready for the full system? start with her wardrobe."

4. **$297+ — "The Director's Room"** *(later, when core is selling)*
   Small-group or 1:1 creative direction. Exists partly to anchor the $97
   as cheap.

**Funnel flow:** Comment SCENE → DM automation delivers free drop + email
capture → email sequence (3 proof posts, then the $17 offer) → checkout
order bump → post-purchase upsell to core / membership. Every path has a
next step — Rachel's rule.

## 4 · Conversion checklist for the store page itself

- [ ] Free item sits FIRST; prices ascend down the page, all ending in 7.
- [ ] Every paid product's page: transformation headline → 3 bullets of
      what changes for them → proof (screenshots of your character's
      comments/views) → what's inside → guarantee → FAQ.
- [ ] Order bump on every checkout. Upsell after every purchase.
- [ ] Copy sounds like a text to a friend, not a brochure.
- [ ] Your character IS the store's imagery — her face on every product
      card (competitors use generic AI girls; yours is a recognizable
      person across the whole store).
- [ ] Post → keyword → DM → email → offer. Never "link in bio" without a
      keyword hook.
