# THE SCENE LOCK-IN™ — Sales Partner Scorecard
### PRIVATE · Internal only · Do not share with applicants

This is how we choose the inaugural six. We are not convincing people to spend $30K. We are finding six founders the Lock-In was built for.

---

## Who we're looking for

> **An established, visible founder with a proven offer, meaningful customer value, the financial capacity to responsibly invest $30K, evidence that she already shows up publicly, a content bottleneck AI can materially solve, and a clear system for continuing execution after the residency.**

**Why this matters:** the first six are not just customers. They are the **proof cohort.** Their content has to go live after Lake Oconee, and their results sell every future Lock-In. The wrong $30K buyer costs more than an empty seat.

**Priority leads:** med spa / aesthetics and beauty founders.

**Also welcome if they're a strong fit:** high-ticket coaches and consultants, established creators, luxury realtors and team leaders, and other visually driven founders.

---

## The process

| Step | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Application review | Sales partner | Score Gate 1 from the application. Decline, waitlist, or invite to a call within 3 business days. |
| 2. Discovery call (30–45 min) | Sales partner | Confirm Gate 1. Score Gate 2. Take first notes on Gate 3. |
| 3. Final call (15–20 min) | Destyni | Score Gate 3. Make the final decision. |
| 4. Acceptance | Destyni / sales partner | Send the acceptance message. The seat is held for 5 business days. |
| 5. Seat secured | Applicant | Signs agreements and pays $15,000 (or $30,000 in full). |
| 6. Onboarding | Event producer | Welcome packet, SCENE FILE intake, travel windows. |

**Final approval is Destyni's.** A high score earns a call with Destyni. It does not earn a seat. If Destyni gets on the call and knows she doesn't want to live with this woman for seven days, that counts, and she doesn't have to justify it.

The one limit: **Destyni doesn't accept someone who failed a must-have** without writing down why. This keeps the whole team honest when someone is exciting on the call.

## Gate 1 · Business fit
*Can she win from this? Scored from the application and confirmed on the discovery call.*

### Must-haves (any "no" = decline)

| Must-have | Pass looks like | Application Qs |
|---|---|---|
| **Proven offer** | She is selling it now, has paying customers, and is amplifying something that works. She is not still figuring out what the business is. | 9–12 |
| **Financial capacity** | $30K is a responsible investment, not a stretch. A useful guide is $40K+ months, or a high-ticket offer plus real cash reserves. **Revenue is a signal, not a hard cutoff.** | 14, 27 |
| **Shows up publicly** | She already posts or goes on camera (posts, lives, podcasts, stories), even if not consistently, and she's open to an AI twin. | 16–19 |
| **AI use case** | There's a real content bottleneck the week can solve, and a believable path from better content to revenue (high customer value, or volume). | 13, 21–23 |

### Scored signals (1–5 each · 25 points)

| Signal | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Offer strength** | New, untested, or changing | Selling steadily, some proof | Proven, in demand, clear results |
| **Financial comfort** | Would strain her or the business | Doable with planning | Easy decision from cash or budget |
| **Visibility** | Rarely posts, dreads the camera | Posts sometimes, open to more | Posts regularly and enjoys it |
| **Revenue path** | Unclear how content makes money | Plausible path | Obvious: high customer value or proven volume |
| **Investing history** | Has never invested meaningfully in growth | Has invested at $3K–$10K | Has invested at $10K+ and can say what she got from it |

**Investing history is a signal, not a requirement.** Someone who has never bought a $30K mastermind can still be perfect. What matters is that she understands investing for leverage and can make the decision without it destabilizing her finances.

**Segment bonus:** +2 for med spa / aesthetics or beauty. +1 for other visually driven businesses.

**To pass Gate 1:** all four must-haves pass **and** 18+ out of 25.

## Gate 2 · Execution fit
*Will the content actually get built and go live? Scored on the discovery call.*

### Must-haves (any "no" = decline or defer to a future cohort)

| Must-have | Pass looks like | Application Qs |
|---|---|---|
| **Can attend the full week** | She has confirmed the dates, with coverage for clients, team, kids, and home. | 33 |
| **Someone owns publishing** | She has a believable answer to "Who is responsible for getting this content published once you leave?" A VA today isn't required. A specific person, or a specific plan and habit, is. "I'll figure it out" doesn't pass. | 25–26 |

### Scored signals (1–5 each · 15 points)

| Signal | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Publishing plan** | Vague, or "I'll figure it out" | She owns it herself and has some habit | A named person or team with hours set aside |
| **Coverage for the week** | Still has to arrange a lot | Mostly arranged | Fully covered, no pull back home |
| **Pre-work readiness** | Unlikely to finish the intake on time | Will get it done | Organized, and has brand assets ready to send |

**To pass Gate 2:** both must-haves pass **and** 11+ out of 15, with **Publishing plan at 3 or higher.**

---

## Gate 3 · Room fit
*Will six founders be better off with her in the house? First notes from the sales partner, final score from Destyni.*

### Automatic red flags (any one = decline)

- **Desperation energy:** "this is my last shot," or needing the week to save the business
- **Paying with money she can't afford to lose,** or credit she can't clear
- **Entitlement:** treats staff poorly, expects personal service beyond the offer, or pushes on price and terms in a way that signals trouble
- **Hates being visible** and wants to disappear behind the brand
- **No real offer:** wants help deciding what the business should be
- Anything that could **disrupt six women living and working together**

### Scored signals (1–5 each · 15 points)

| Signal | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coachable** | Defensive, knows best | Open to direction | Asks for feedback and acts on it |
| **Collaborative** | Only about her | Pleasant, keeps to herself | Generous: celebrates others, shares what she knows |
| **Professional** | Disorganized, late, or dramatic | Reliable | Calm, prepared, respectful of everyone's time |

**To pass Gate 3:** no red flags, 12+ out of 15, no signal below 3, **and Destyni says yes.**

## Scoring summary

| | Points | Pass mark |
|---|---|---|
| Gate 1 · Business fit | 25 (+2 bonus) | All must-haves + 18 |
| Gate 2 · Execution fit | 15 | All must-haves + 11, Publishing plan ≥ 3 |
| Gate 3 · Room fit | 15 | No red flags + 12, nothing below 3, Destyni yes |
| **Total** | **55 (+2)** | Pass all three gates |

If more than six founders pass, **rank by total score, then Destyni chooses the mix.** Aim for a room that works together: a mix of businesses that can learn from each other, with no one who would be isolated.

**Decisions after each gate:**
- **Advance:** passes the gate
- **Waitlist:** a strong founder with a timing issue (dates, cash timing, publishing support not ready yet). Invite her to the next cohort.
- **Decline:** failed a must-have, has a red flag, or is too far from fit

## Discovery call guide (sales partner)

**Opening (2 min):** Thank her. Explain that this is a two-way conversation to see if it's a fit, that seats are limited to six, and that Destyni makes the final choice.

### Gate 1 · Business fit
- "Walk me through your business: what you sell, who buys it, and what a client is worth to you."
- "What's working in your marketing right now? What isn't?"
- "What are you launching or pushing in the next 90 days?"
- "If you left with 30 days of content done, what happens in your business?"
- "How are you thinking about the investment? Where would it come from?"
- "What's the biggest investment you've made in your growth? What did you get from it?"

### Gate 2 · Execution fit
- **"Who is responsible for getting this content published once you leave?"** *(Push for a name or a specific plan.)*
- "What does your week look like if you're gone for seven days? Who covers clients, team, and home?"
- "The residency starts 2–3 weeks before, with a detailed intake and your brand assets. Can you get that to us on time?"

### Gate 3 · Room fit (first notes)
- "Tell me about a group program or mastermind you've been in. What did you bring to it?"
- "What would you want from the other five founders in the house?"
- "What would make this week a disappointment for you?" *(Listen for unrealistic expectations.)*

**Close:** "Thank you. I'll share my notes with Destyni. If it's a fit, the next step is a short call with her. Either way, you'll hear from us within [X] business days."

**Never on this call:** send a checkout link, promise a seat, discount the price, or promise specific results.

---

## Destyni's final call (15–20 min)

This call is about chemistry, standards, and vision. It isn't a sales pitch.

- "What made you apply?"
- "What do you want to be known for a year from now?"
- "How do you feel about building a digital version of yourself?"
- "What kind of energy do you bring into a shared space?"
- **Destyni's gut check:** *Do I want to live and work with her for seven days? Will the other five be better off with her there?*
